About Youth Sports Network NB

A non-profit organization dedicated to the health, development, and belonging of every young person in New Brunswick.

Who We Are

Youth Sports Network NB (YSN-NB) is a community-driven non-profit organization based in New Brunswick, Canada. We believe every young person — regardless of background, ability, or economic situation — deserves access to sport, community, and opportunity.

We are committed to making a difference not only on sports courts, but also in the local communities that welcome each of us. Our programs bridge sport, education, and social inclusion to create lasting positive change.

We especially focus on welcoming newcomers and immigrants, helping them integrate into the community through the universal language of sport and play.

Our Core Objectives

  • Provide accessible sport programs for all youth
  • Foster inclusion for newcomers and immigrants
  • Develop mindset, leadership and life skills
  • Partner with schools, colleges & universities
  • Create free community activities at parks
  • Launch competitive youth sports teams
  • Build a healthier, more connected NB

Kids in Motion

Free activities for youth at parks & playgrounds

Outdoor Fun — At No Cost

Kids in Motion is our flagship free community program, bringing organized activities directly to parks and playgrounds across New Brunswick. No registration fees. No barriers. Just fun, fitness, and friendship.

Activities include organized sports, competitive games, fitness demonstrations, guided nature walks, board games, water games, and seasonal activities — something for everyone.

Beacon Youth Program

Beacon is our mindset and development-oriented school-based after-school program. In partnership with colleges and universities, we prepare youth for the next generation — building the skills, confidence, and resilience they need to succeed.

Sessions take place evenings, weekends, and holidays — designed to fit around school schedules and reach the youth who need it most.

Program Focus Areas

  • Mindset & Mental Resilience
  • Leadership & Communication
  • Academic Support & Mentorship
  • Career Exploration & Life Skills
  • College & University Partnerships

Community & Inclusion
