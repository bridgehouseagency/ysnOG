Our Mission

What We Stand For

We are committed to making a difference — not only on sports courts, but also in the local community that welcomes each of us.

Active Youth

Providing free and accessible sports, fitness, and recreational programs for children and youth across New Brunswick.

Inclusive Community

Building bridges between newcomers, immigrants, and local communities through the universal language of sport and play.

Youth Development

Preparing the next generation with mindset, leadership, and life skills through academic partnerships and mentorship programs.

Newcomers & Immigrants

Sport Unites Us All

We focus on welcoming newcomers and immigrants into our programs to help them better integrate into the local community alongside their neighbours.

Through sport and shared experiences, language and cultural barriers fade. Every child deserves to play, belong, and thrive — no matter where they come from.

All Are Welcome Here

From every corner of the world, every culture and background is celebrated in our programs. We are stronger together.
