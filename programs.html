Kids in Motion

Free activities for youth at parks & playgrounds

100% Free — No Registration Fee
Community Program

Outdoor Fun — At No Cost

Kids in Motion is our flagship free community program, bringing organized activities directly to parks and playgrounds across New Brunswick. No registration fees. No barriers. Just fun, fitness, and friendship.

Activities include organized sports, competitive games, fitness demonstrations, guided nature walks, board games, water games, and seasonal activities — something for everyone.

After School Program

Compass Youth Program

Compass is our mindset and development-oriented school-based after-school program. In partnership with colleges and universities, we prepare youth for the next generation — building the skills, confidence, and resilience they need to succeed.

Sessions take place evenings, weekends, and holidays — designed to fit around school schedules and reach the youth who need it most.

Program Focus Areas

  • Mindset & Mental Resilience
  • Leadership & Communication
  • Academic Support & Mentorship
  • Career Exploration & Life Skills
  • College & University Partnerships
