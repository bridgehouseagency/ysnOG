Kids in Motion

Free activities for youth at parks & playgrounds

100% Free — No Registration Fee
Community Program

Outdoor Fun — At No Cost

Kids in Motion is our flagship free community program, bringing organized activities directly to parks and playgrounds across New Brunswick. No registration fees. No barriers. Just fun, fitness, and friendship.

Activities include organized sports, competitive games, fitness demonstrations, guided nature walks, board games, water games, and seasonal activities — something for everyone.

Compass Youth Program

Guiding our athletes toward what comes next

After-School Program
Development Program

Helping Youth Look Beyond the Game

Compass helps our athletes succeed beyond the game — pairing every youth in our sports programs with new connections and a clear path toward life after high school.

Sessions run evenings, weekends, and summer holidays — fitting around school schedules to reach the youth who need it most.

How Compass Helps

Connection & Networking

Youth forums and gatherings connect our athletes with peers from other regions and across Canada — opening minds, building friendships, and broadening their sense of what's possible.

College & Career Exploration

Through partnerships with colleges and universities, our youth visit campuses, meet students and faculty, and explore career paths — building a real picture of what comes after high school.

Tech Access & Innovation

In partnership with local tech hubs, youth get early exposure to technology — attending conferences, seminars, and hands-on workshops that open doors to future careers in tech.
