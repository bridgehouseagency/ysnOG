Youth Teams

Two exciting new youth teams launching Summer 2027. All passionate young athletes are welcome to try out.

“Harambe”

A Swahili word meaning “Let's Pull Together.”

Why “Harambe”?

More Than a Name

We chose the name Harambe because it captures exactly what our teams stand for. This is not just about soccer or basketball — it's about young people coming together, pulling in the same direction, and building something bigger than themselves.

Our team is not about sports — it is about:

Unity

Coming together as one team, one community, pulling in the same direction toward a shared goal.

Belonging

Creating a space where every young person feels welcomed, valued, and like they are part of something meaningful.

Resilience

Building the strength and determination to push through challenges — on the field, and in life.

Opportunity

Opening doors for young athletes to grow, compete, and discover what they are capable of achieving.

Representation

Making sure every young person — including newcomers and youth from all backgrounds — sees themselves reflected and represented on our teams.

Youth Empowerment

Giving young people the confidence, skills, and support they need to lead, grow, and shape their own futures.

Tryout Process

Register online, receive a confirmation email with tryout date and location, show up and give it your all. All skill levels welcome — we evaluate effort and attitude as much as skill.

Open to Everyone

Our teams are open to all youth regardless of background, experience, or whether you are a newcomer to Canada. Sport is a universal language.

Timeline

Tryout registrations open early 2027. Teams officially launch Summer 2027. Register your interest now and we'll notify you as soon as tryout dates are confirmed.
