About Youth Sports Network NB

A non-profit organization dedicated to the health, development, and belonging of every young person in New Brunswick.

Who We Are

Our Story

Youth Sports Network NB (YSN-NB) is a community-driven non-profit organization based in New Brunswick, Canada. We believe every young person — regardless of background, ability, or economic situation — deserves access to sport, community, and opportunity.

We are committed to making a difference not only on sports courts, but also in the local communities that welcome each of us. Our programs bridge sport, education, and social inclusion to create lasting positive change.

We especially focus on welcoming newcomers and immigrants, helping them integrate into the community through the universal language of sport and play.

Our Core Objectives

  • Provide accessible sport programs for all youth
  • Foster inclusion for newcomers and immigrants
  • Develop a competitive mindset among youth
  • Connect athletes to colleges & universities for career exploration
  • Create free community activities at parks
  • Launch competitive youth sports teams
  • Build strong and connected youth
